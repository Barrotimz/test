Three places to stop. The ask margin is what the listing shows. The net is what is left after fees.

Costs assume crafting mastery 100. A combo upgrades the piece on about a quarter of successes, and a miss consumes the materials, so one session swings around the average.

The world preset uses a 20% sale fee and a 2% listing fee, the rates community calculators use for the world exchange. The server preset uses 10% and 2%. Set both to match the exchange you actually list on.

The exit is the lower of the cheapest ask and the 28-day average sale. Listings and sales come from Aion2Market, which snapshots the exchange about once an hour. Nothing here is a guarantee a buyer takes the item.
