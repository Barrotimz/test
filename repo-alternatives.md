# Alternatives to R.E.P.O.

R.E.P.O. (semiwork, Early Access, $9.99, up to 6 players) is physics-based co-op extraction horror: haul fragile loot, whisper on proximity chat, and wipe in slapstick. These are the closest replacements as of August 2026, grouped by what you actually liked.

Steam: [R.E.P.O.](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3241660/REPO/)

## Closest matches

### Lethal Company — $9.99, 1–4 players
The template R.E.P.O. built on. Scavenge scrap on abandoned moons, hit a rising quota, extract before the night shift eats you. Darker and first-person; no physics gun. Best mod catalog in the genre.

[Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1966720/Lethal_Company/)

### GRAIN ROT — $8.99 intro / $9.99, 1–4 players (out Aug 7, 2026)
Newest physics-extraction cousin. Ride a cursed elevator into shifting ruins, rip furniture, haul loot, then rebuild a persistent outpost. Wooden bodies shatter; you keep going as a Spark. Closest *feel* to fumbling valuables with friends.

[Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4450620/GRAIN_ROT/)

### Content Warning — ~$8, 1–4 players
Landfall’s SpookTube twist: film monsters for views instead of selling scrap. Same descend-and-extract nights, same proximity-chat disasters, goofier and shorter. Best “our group just wants to laugh” pick.

[Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2881650/Content_Warning/)

### Murky Divers — ~$9, 1–8 players
Underwater quota run. Pilot a sub, dive wrecks, grab evidence/treasure, get out. Physics handling plus a bigger lobby than R.E.P.O.’s 6. Best if your Discord is too big for Lethal Company.

[Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2963880/Murky_Divers/)

### DREGS — Early Access (Jul 17, 2026), 1–8 players
Clone crew recovering relics from space derelicts. Horror-comedy where friends and miscommunication kill you faster than the monsters. Another 8-player extraction option if Murky Divers is too wet.

[Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4660920/DREGS/)

## Same chaos, different loop

### PEAK — $7.99, 1–4 players
Co-op climbing, not horror. One bad jump still wipes the night. Proximity chat, daily-rotating mountain, friends-only lobbies. Play this when the group is tired of monsters but not of yelling.

[Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3527290/PEAK/)

### PANICORE — ~$10, up to 5 players
Escape a facility without talking above a whisper. The monster hears real proximity voice and footsteps. No loot quota; all panic.

[Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2695940/PANICORE/)

### Phasmophobia — ~$20, 1–4 players
Voice-reactive ghost hunting. Slower, scarier, more systems. No physics hauling. Best long-term “serious scare” replacement, with VR.

[Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/739630/Phasmophobia/)

### DEVOUR — ~$5–$10, 1–4 players
Short ritual rounds: gather items, finish the rite, don’t get caught. Arcade pacing, frequent wipes. Good for 30-minute sessions.

[Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1274570/DEVOUR/)

## Heavier / more polished

### Deep Rock Galactic — often ~$9 on sale, 1–4 players
Mine, haul, extract, shoot. Destructible caves and friendly fire instead of haunted houses. Most complete “bring the stuff back alive” game on this list.

[Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/548430/Deep_Rock_Galactic/)

### The Outlast Trials — often ~$12 on sale, 1–4 players
Cold-war trials: carry objectives while stalkers hunt you. Same extract-or-die shape, almost no comedy.

[Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1304930/The_Outlast_Trials/)

### GTFO — often cheap on sale, 1–4 players
Hardcore stealth shooter in a dark complex. Sound discipline, scarce ammo, almost zero slapstick. For squads that found R.E.P.O. too forgiving.

[Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/493520/GTFO/)

## Coming up

**V.A.U.L.T. PROTOCOL** (Early Access planned Aug 21, 2026) — 1–6 player nuclear-wasteland extraction for an AI overseer. Same player count as R.E.P.O.; loot raises the threat level until you extract or get recycled.

[Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4178200/VAULT_PROTOCOL/)

## Quick picks by lobby size

- **4 players:** Lethal Company, Content Warning, GRAIN ROT, PEAK, Phasmophobia
- **5–6 players:** R.E.P.O. itself, PANICORE (5), V.A.U.L.T. PROTOCOL (6, soon)
- **8 players:** Murky Divers, DREGS

Prices move on Steam sales. Most of these sit under $15 at full price.
